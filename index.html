life

A short document about the ordinary interval between arriving and understanding.

## begin

You arrive without instructions. No one hands you a map, and the ones who try are reading from their own. For a while you assume the missing page is coming. Then you stop assuming.

The early years go into learning the language of rooms — which silences mean danger, which doors stay open, where to stand. You get good at it. You mistake the skill for a self.

Then, on no date anyone marks, you begin. You wake up inside a life that is yours, and you have opinions about it. That is the whole ceremony. There is no one to hand you a certificate.

## drift

Most of it is maintenance. You feed the body, pay the bills, answer the message, replace the thing that broke. The hours can be accounted for, mostly, and still they go.

Drift is not failure. It is what happens when you stop steering and the current is kind enough to keep you afloat. You look up and a year has passed in the shape of small habits.

You will not remember most of these days. That is not the same as not living them.

The days do not announce themselves as the ones you will remember. Most arrive without weather, without a decision, without anything that would make them worth marking.

The work is not to escape the drift. It is to notice when you have been carried, and decide, once, whether you meant to go there.

## notice

You do not get the past back and you do not get to rehearse the future. What is left is the width of a single day, which is wider than it looks.

The day does not ask you to deserve it.

Attention is the only currency that does not inflate. Spend it on the person in front of you, the meal you are actually eating, the walk you take without a destination. None of this is noble. It is just where the life is.

There is no version of attention that can be stored for later. It spends as it is used.

## end

Nothing resolves. You do not become finished, and the people around you do not become finished either. There is no final version of you waiting at the end of the corrections. The draft is the work.

So you keep going. You get some things right, you repeat some mistakes, you love people who are also making it up. The file stays open. The cursor blinks.

That is not a failure of the story. That is the story.
